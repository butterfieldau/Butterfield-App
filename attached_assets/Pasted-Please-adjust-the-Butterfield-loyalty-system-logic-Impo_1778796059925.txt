Please adjust the Butterfield loyalty system logic.

Important:
The customer QR code is only for marking off coffees when the customer buys coffee in-store.

The QR should not control general loyalty points.

QR / coffee stamp rules:
- Every customer must automatically get their own unique QR code when they create an account.
- The QR code must be personal to that customer only.
- The QR code should be available from both the Home Screen QR button and the Rewards / Loyalty page.
- Staff, managers and directors should be able to scan the QR from the backend.
- When the QR is scanned, the backend should load that customer’s coffee stamp card.
- Staff can then mark off coffee purchases manually.
- 1 paid coffee = 1 coffee stamp.
- Once the customer reaches 6 coffee stamps, they receive 1 free coffee reward.
- When the free coffee is redeemed, the coffee stamp count should reset or deduct correctly.
- Keep a backend history of all coffee stamp actions, including customer ID, staff ID, date/time and action type.
- The QR code should not expose the customer’s name, email or phone number. It should only use a secure loyalty QR token.

In-app loyalty points rules:
- Loyalty points are only for purchases made through the app.
- When a customer places an order in the app, points should automatically be added to their account.
- Points should show correctly on the Home Screen, Rewards screen and customer account.
- Points should not be added from staff scanning the QR.
- QR scans should only affect coffee stamps, not points.
- If an in-app order is cancelled, refunded or failed, the points from that order must be removed.
- Prevent duplicate points from being added if the same order is processed twice.

Coffee purchases through the app:
- If a customer buys coffee through the app, this should also add coffee stamps automatically.
- Example: customer buys 2 coffees in the app = 2 coffee stamps added.
- This should happen without needing staff to scan the QR.
- If that order is refunded or cancelled, remove those coffee stamps.
