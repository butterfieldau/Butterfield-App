Build a complete Customer Profile Management system in the Butterfield app backend.
Every customer should have a detailed profile that staff and directors can view and manage. This is important for smooth operations, delivery, wholesale management, loyalty, customer service and future marketing.
Customer Profile Details
Each customer profile should include:
Personal Details
Full name
Email address
Phone number
Date joined
Customer type: Retail / Wholesale / Staff / VIP
Account status: Active / Inactive / Suspended
Notes section for internal staff use
Delivery Details
Primary delivery address
Option to add multiple saved addresses
Suburb
Postcode
Delivery instructions
Preferred delivery window
Pickup or delivery preference
Billing Details
Billing name
Billing address
ABN / business details for wholesale customers
Invoice email
Payment terms for wholesale customers
Outstanding balance
Paid invoices
Unpaid invoices
Card on File
Card on file should only be available for approved wholesale customers
Do not store raw card details directly in the app
Use a secure payment provider token system only
Show card brand, last 4 digits and expiry date
Allow directors to enable or disable card-on-file access
Allow wholesale customers to approve saved payment method usage
Order History
Total number of orders
Total amount spent
Average order value
Last order date
Most ordered products
Cancelled orders
Refunded orders
Wholesale order history
Retail order history
Filter orders by date, status, customer type and product
Customer Ranking / Loyalty Status
Automatically rank customers based on their ordering activity.
Example ranking levels:
New Customer: 1 order
Returning Customer: 2–4 orders
Loyal Customer: 5–14 orders
VIP Customer: 15+ orders or high spend
Wholesale Partner: approved wholesale account
High Value Customer: based on total spend
The backend should clearly show badges on each customer profile, such as:
Loyal Customer
VIP
Wholesale Partner
High Spend
Frequent Buyer
Needs Follow Up
Inactive Customer
Wholesale Customer Features
Wholesale profiles should include:
Business name
ABN
Business address
Delivery address
Contact person
Phone number
Email
Pricing tier
Approved products
Minimum order quantities
Payment terms
Credit limit
Card on file status
Invoice history
Order frequency
Notes from directors
Assigned staff member or account manager
Director Controls
Directors should be able to:
View all customers
Search customers by name, phone, email, business or suburb
Filter by Retail, Wholesale, VIP, Loyal Customer, Inactive, High Spend
Edit customer details
Add internal notes
Change customer ranking manually if needed
Approve or reject wholesale accounts
Assign pricing tiers
View customer lifetime value
View customer order patterns
Export customer data to CSV
Send customer offers or reminders
Flag customers for follow-up
Staff Access
Staff should only see what they need for operations:
Customer name
Phone number
Order history
Delivery address
Delivery instructions
Pickup notes
Internal notes related to orders
Staff should not be able to see sensitive billing or payment information unless permission is given by a director.
Customer Insights Dashboard
Add a dashboard that shows:
Total customers
New customers this week
Returning customers
Loyal customers
VIP customers
Wholesale customers
Inactive customers
Top spending customers
Most frequent customers
Customers who have not ordered in 30 days
Customers with unpaid wholesale invoices
Automation / Smart Features
Add smart suggestions such as:
“This customer has ordered 10 times, mark as Loyal Customer”
“This customer has not ordered in 30 days, send offer”
“This wholesale customer usually orders weekly but has not ordered this week”
“This customer spends above average”
“This customer may be suitable for VIP rewards”
Privacy and Security
Make sure customer data is secure.
Role-based access control
Directors can see everything
Managers can see limited customer and order details
Staff can only see operational details
Payment details must be tokenised and never stored directly
Log all changes made to customer profiles
Only directors can delete or export customer data
The system should feel professional, clean and easy to use, similar to a serious POS / CRM system built for a growing food and wholesale business.
The goal is to help Butterfield understand every customer properly, improve service, manage wholesale customers smoothly, reward loyal customers and operate at a high level.
