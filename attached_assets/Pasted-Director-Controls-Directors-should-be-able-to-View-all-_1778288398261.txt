Director Controls
Directors should be able to:
View all customers
Search customers by name, phone, email, business or suburb
Filter by Retail, Wholesale, VIP, Loyal Customer, Inactive, High Spend
Edit customer details
Add internal notes
Change customer ranking manually if needed
Approve or reject wholesale accounts
Assign pricing tiers
View customer lifetime value
View customer order patterns
Export customer data to CSV
Send customer offers or reminders
Flag customers for follow-up
Staff Access
Staff should only see what they need for operations:
Customer name
Phone number
Order history
Delivery address
Delivery instructions
Pickup notes
Internal notes related to orders
Staff should not be able to see sensitive billing or payment information unless permission is given by a director.
Customer Insights Dashboard
Add a dashboard that shows:
Total customers
New customers this week
Returning customers
Loyal customers
VIP customers
Wholesale customers
Inactive customers
Top spending customers
Most frequent customers
Customers who have not ordered in 30 days
Customers with unpaid wholesale invoices
Automation / Smart Features
Add smart suggestions such as:
“This customer has ordered 10 times, mark as Loyal Customer”
“This customer has not ordered in 30 days, send offer”
“This wholesale customer usually orders weekly but has not ordered this week”
“This customer spends above average”
“This customer may be suitable for VIP rewards”
Privacy and Security
Make sure customer data is secure.
Role-based access control
Directors can see everything
Managers can see limited customer and order details
Staff can only see operational details
Payment details must be tokenised and never stored directly
Log all changes made to customer profiles
Only directors can delete or export customer data
The system should feel professional, clean and easy to use, similar to a serious POS / CRM system built for a growing food and wholesale business.
The goal is to help Butterfield understand every customer properly, improve service, manage wholesale customers smoothly, reward loyal customers and operate at a high level.
