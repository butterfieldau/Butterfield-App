Replace the fixed top header message with a dynamic personalised message system.
Current example:

The app should automatically change the message based on:
Customer name
Time of day
Day of the week
Australian seasons
Public holidays and special days
Weather
Customer behaviour
Store opening hours
Promotions
Loyalty points
Birthday
Last order history
The message should feel warm, premium, personal and natural. Not robotic.
